I am an incoming first-year Astronomy PhD student at the University of Edinburgh.  My main interests include exoplanetary research and instrumentation. I am currently working on high-contrast imaging of exoplanets, with a focus in giant planet and brown dwarf demographics with JWST and HST. Additionally, I have research experience in solar physics, cosmology, and planetary science.
