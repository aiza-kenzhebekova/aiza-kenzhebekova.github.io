Hi! I am a first year astronomy PhD student at the University of Edinburgh where I study the demographics of directly imaged exoplanets and brown dwarfs. 

My main focus is finding and characterising exoplanets using JWST and HST, with the goal of learning more about the exoplanet population as a whole. Additionally, I have research experience in solar physics, cosmology, and planetary science.

I completed my MPhys degree at the University of Edinburgh. During this time, I created a tool that determines the probability that a candidate exoplanet is a background object based on its colour, magnitude, and separation from its host star for a variety of JWST filter. Learn more about [SIDERIS here](https://github.com/aiza-kenzhebekova/SIDERIS).
