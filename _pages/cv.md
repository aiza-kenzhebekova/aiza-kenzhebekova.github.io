Research experience
======

* MPhys Project: [SIDERIS](https://github.com/aiza-kenzhebekova/SIDERIS)
  * Sept 2025 - April 2026 
  * Developed a tool that calculates the background object occurrence probability in JWST direct imaging observations.
  * Used the JADES and SMILES galaxy catalogues, as well as TRILEGAL simulations to obtain the
expectation value of background objects.
  * Supervisor: Prof Beth Biller, Dr Ben Suttlieff




* Telescope Group Project: Characterisation of the Physical and Orbital Properties of the Transiting Exoplanet HD 189733b
  * Sept 2024 - Feb 2025
  * Collected transit data using a remote telescope (PIRATE)
  * Performed data reduction and aperture photometry (Source Extractor) to obtain a light curve, and fit for orbital and physical parameters using the BATMAN python package, accounting for different limb darkening models.
  * Determined the spectral type of the host star using observations of reference stars in different filters
  * Supervisor: Prof Trent Dupuy



    
* Senior Honours Project: The Future is Now: Weak Gravitational Lensing Cosmology with Euclid
  * Autumn 2024
  * Used Euclid’s Flagship Simulation to find the difference in redshift distributions of galaxies from DES and UNIONS and measure its impact on constraints of the $s_8$ cosmological parameter.
  * Found a bias in the lensing power spectrum that resulted in a > 3$\sigma$ variation in $s_8$.
  * Supervisor: Dr Benjamin Giblin




* Research Intern (National Solar Observatory, University of Colorado, Boulder)
  * Summer 2024
  * Conducted solar research using multi-instrument views of the Sun in the hydrogen-alpha line.
  * Utilised Python and other scientific software to process large datasets and visualise solar dynamics.
  * Presented my findings as a presentation and poster at a symposium.
  * Supervisor: Prof Kevin Reardon, Sarah Bruce
